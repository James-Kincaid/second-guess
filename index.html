    How it works

    What is the model actually doing?

    It reads the text so far and gives every piece of text it knows a score: how likely that piece is to come next. Then it picks one at random, weighted by those scores. That's the whole trick, repeated one piece at a time.

    Why are some choices only part of a word?

    Models don't read whole words. They read tokens: common words, chunks of longer words, and punctuation. "Unbelievable" might be three tokens. A dot at the start of a choice means it begins with a space, which makes it a new word.

    What is heat?

    Heat is what AI builders call temperature. When it's low, the model almost always picks its favorite. When it's high, the odds even out and long shots come in more often. Win a round and the heat goes up. Lose one and it cools off.

    How is the score worked out?

    You score more the bigger the share of your chips on the piece the model picked. Spreading evenly scores about zero. Going all in scores big if you're right and loses big if you're wrong. Over time, the best strategy is to bet the way the model really thinks.
